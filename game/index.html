VOID BLOOM

You are the last Bloomkeeper. Shapes of the Void pour in, wave after wave, to snuff out the garden. Shatter them, harvest their golden pollen, and spend it on new blooms — twin shots, thorn pierce, an aegis bud — before the dark closes in.
